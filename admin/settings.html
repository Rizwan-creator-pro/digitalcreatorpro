Visitor Location ?Controls how precisely visits are located on the Analytics map.

Ask visitors for their exact location (browser GPS permission prompt)
Off (default): every visit is located from its IP address only — no prompt, no permission needed. This gives the nearest city, not an exact point.
On: the browser also asks the visitor to share their precise location. If they allow it, that visit uses GPS-accurate coordinates instead. If they deny it, ignore it, or their browser doesn't support it, the visit still gets logged — it just falls back to the IP-based nearest location automatically. Nothing is ever skipped either way.
